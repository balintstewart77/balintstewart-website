Show what your data is used for, and the public benefit it delivers.

I turn public registers of data use into searchable, validated evidence of public benefit: for funding renewals, for public accountability, and for deciding where to invest.

Who it’s for

  • Trusted research environments and data custodians: show how your data is used, demonstrate public benefit, and publish transparent, searchable registers.
  • Funders: see which investments researchers actually use, by whom and for what, and where demand isn’t being met.
  • Public bodies: account for public funding with clear evidence of what research it supports.

Services

  1. Data use dashboards. Turn a static register of approved projects into a live, searchable dashboard, with each project classified by topic and purpose.
  2. Portfolio insight for funders. Analysis of research activity across your investments: what’s used, what’s growing, and where activity matches or misses your priorities.
  3. Trustworthy AI classification. Where AI is used to classify or summarise records, a validation plan that tests it against trained human judgement, so you know which results you can rely on.

Proof

A live public dashboard of over 1,300 projects accredited under the Digital Economy Act, with a pre-registered validation study.

How it works

A typical engagement takes six weeks.

  1. Agree the questions that matter to you.
  2. Clean and structure your register.
  3. Build the dashboard and taxonomy.
  4. Validate, then hand over.
